Synced Lyric Extractor

Load an mp3, load lyrics, tap along to the beat, export timecodes.

🔒 Your mp3 and lyrics never leave your device. This tool has no server — nothing you load is uploaded, copied, or transmitted anywhere, and nobody (including us) can see or access your files. Everything happens locally, in your browser's own memory. The only thing saved anywhere is your tagged progress, autosaved to your browser's local storage on this device (so an accidental reload doesn't lose it) — never sent out. Copy/Download only ever save to your own computer. Don't just take our word for it: this page is a handful of plain, readable files (view the source) with a strict browser security policy that blocks it from ever contacting any external server — you can check both yourself.
